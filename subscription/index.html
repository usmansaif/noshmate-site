Subscription FAQ

Billing & Flexibility

What's the difference between the Weekly and Monthly plan?
Same rotating menu and delivery either way — the only difference is how often you're billed. Weekly suits teams that want to review usage often; Monthly means one invoice instead of four.
Can I pause or skip a day?
Yes — message us on WhatsApp ahead of the day you want to skip and we'll adjust your plan. There's no app to manage it in, it's a direct message.
What's the ordering cutoff for subscription lunches?
Subscription lunches are pre-scheduled off your plan, so there's nothing to order daily — the 11 AM cutoff only applies if you're adding an extra one-off order on top of your plan.
