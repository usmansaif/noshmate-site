Our Story

Why We Started NoshMaté

NoshMaté began with a simple frustration — office lunch in Lahore usually meant long queues, cold food, or the same three restaurants on rotation. So we built a kitchen that cooks fresh every morning and a delivery routine tight enough to hit desks before the lunch break ends.

What started with a handful of offices in Gulberg now feeds teams across DHA, Johar Town, Model Town and beyond — one hot tray at a time.

Our Mission

Make dependable, home-style lunch accessible to every office in Lahore — without the wait, the guesswork, or the markup.

Our Vision

To be the lunch order every office in Lahore reaches for by default.

What is NoshMaté?
NoshMaté is a daily lunch delivery service for offices in Lahore. We cook fresh every day and deliver hot meals straight to your desk.
Do you deliver every working day?
Yes — we deliver Monday to Saturday across all the areas listed in our coverage section above.
Can I set up a recurring weekly plan?
Yes. Message us on WhatsApp to set up a Weekly Lunch Plan for yourself or your whole team, billed once a week.

Every batch of ingredients is checked before it enters the kitchen, and every tray is checked again before it leaves for delivery.
